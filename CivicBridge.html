About CivicBridge

Most people miss schemes they already qualify for, not because they don’t need them, but because nobody told them in a language and a format they can use. CivicBridge closes that gap: describe your situation by typing or speaking, in your language, even on a slow connection.

The gap we’re closing

800M+Indians eligible for at least one unclaimed scheme

Indicative estimates, shown to convey scale.

How it works

  1. Tell us about youAnswer a few simple questions, or just talk to the AI guide in your language.
  2. See what fitsSchemes are ranked by how well they match, with the reason for each.
  3. Walk in preparedGet the documents to carry, the office to visit and a helpline to call.

Who it helps

FarmersWomen and mothersYoung job seekersDaily-wage workersFamilies without a bank accountFirst-time home seekers

What it does

  • 🔎Eligibility matcher: six questions give a ranked list with reasons and a document checklist
  • 🤖AI guide: describe your situation in plain words and get matched schemes
  • 🗣️Voice in and voice out in seven languages (Web Speech API)
  • 📶Low-data mode, offline fallback and no external fonts or images, so it loads on 2G
  • ♿Keyboard friendly, screen-reader labels, visible focus, reduced-motion support
  • 🔌Backend AI (IBM watsonx.ai Granite or OpenAI) with a rule-based fallback when offline

Design principles

No prior knowledge needed. Plain language. Every answer ends with a next step: a helpline or a Common Service Centre. Estimates are guidance only; the final decision rests with the scheme authority.

How it was built

Built with IBM Bob for the AI for Communities, Access & Public Impact track. The AI guide runs on IBM watsonx.ai (Granite) through the backend and falls back to on-device rules when there is no connection.
